# Modulo

Modulo is a personal finance app for iOS and Android, built around the 50/30/20 budgeting method.

## App

- **[Modulo-Mobile](https://github.com/modulo-app/Modulo-Mobile)** — The Modulo app, for iOS and Android.

## Backend

- **[Modulo-Backend](https://github.com/modulo-app/Modulo-Backend)** — All the backend services powering Modulo.
- **[Modulo-Gateway](https://github.com/modulo-app/Modulo-Gateway)** — Routes requests from the app to the right service.
- **[Modulo-Auth](https://github.com/modulo-app/Modulo-Auth)** — Handles sign-in and account security.
- **[Modulo-Users](https://github.com/modulo-app/Modulo-Users)** — Manages user accounts and preferences.
- **[Modulo-Transactions](https://github.com/modulo-app/Modulo-Transactions)** — Tracks your budget across Needs, Wants, and Savings.
- **[Modulo-Subscriptions](https://github.com/modulo-app/Modulo-Subscriptions)** — Tracks your subscriptions and recurring payments.
- **[Modulo-Notifications](https://github.com/modulo-app/Modulo-Notifications)** — Sends reminders and monthly summaries.
- **[Modulo-Common](https://github.com/modulo-app/Modulo-Common)** — Shared code used across services.

## Other

- **[PressKit](https://github.com/modulo-app/PressKit)** — Logos, screenshots, and brand assets for press and media.
